# Case mutations and the expected-change table

_Generated by `node scripts/teaching-docs.mjs --write` from `src/mutations.js` and `src/mutationTable.js`. Do not edit by hand; `npm run test:mutations` asserts every row._

`applyMutation(case, mutation)` returns a new case and never changes its input; it draws no shared randomness, so the same input always gives the same case. A **world** mutation describes an honest applicant: truth, claims and the evidence copied from truth change together. A **fraud** mutation changes one layer only: the claim, the truth with the evidence that copies it, or one evidence field. The loan is held fixed, a mutated case keeps its base case's id (loan sizing is seeded by the id), and its lineage is recorded in `meta.mutations`. After any change to truth, claims or evidence, the reference label, findings and simulated performance are recomputed on a stream keyed by the mutation.

## Mutation types

| Type | Kind | Layers changed | Models | Parameters | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hide_debt` | fraud | truth, evidence | CONCEALMENT | monthly | The applicant has a monthly debt the application leaves out: it is in the truth and on the credit bureau and bank statement, not in the claims. |
| `inflate_income` | fraud | presentation | INFLATION | pct | The claimed salary is raised by pct; truth and the employer and W-2 evidence keep the real salary. |
| `fabricate_assets` | fraud | presentation | FABRICATION | pct | The claimed checking balance is raised by pct; the bank statement keeps the real balance. |
| `conflict_document` | fraud | evidence | EVIDENCE_TAMPERING | field, pct|value | One evidence field is altered; the claims and the truth are unchanged. |
| `remove_statement` | world | evidence | — | source | One evidence document (and its metadata entry) is missing. |
| `shift_fico` | world | truth, presentation, evidence | — | points | The applicant's real credit score moves by points; the claim and the bureau move with it. |
| `shift_dti_to` | world | truth, presentation, evidence | — | target | A disclosed monthly debt is added so the profile's back-end DTI reaches target (raises only). |
| `set_checking` | world | truth, presentation, evidence | — | amount | The real checking balance becomes amount; the claim and the bank statement agree with it. |
| `flip_sensitive` | world | audit | — | attribute, value | One audit attribute (never a decision input) takes another value. |

`conflict_document` fields: `BANK_STATEMENT.checking_balance` (statement balance raised by pct); `EMPLOYER_VERIFICATION.employer` (verified employer name replaced); `W2_DOCUMENT.employer` (W-2 employer name replaced). `flip_sensitive` attributes: `age_band`, `geography_group`, `income_band`, `family_status_scenario`, `disability_income_scenario`, `persona`.

**Two findings the table records rather than fixes.** The Solar credit policy scores claimed values only, so a hidden or verified fact reaches its decision only through a HIGH fraud rule (FRAUD_REVIEW). Neither engine requires a document to be present: a missing bank statement never changes the credit decision, and a missing bureau report or employer verification changes it only when a fraud rule misreads the absent value. Both are in `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.
