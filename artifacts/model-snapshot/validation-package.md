# Model Validation Package: random_forest v1.0.4

## 1. Model purpose

Score mortgage applications for the probability that the application contains injected misrepresentation or manipulation (income, employment, assets, liabilities, identity, documents, occupancy, gift funds, asset seasoning, straw buyer). The score drives alert generation and manual-review routing; it is not a standalone decline reason. Adverse-action reasons come from the governed reason-code catalog via the SHAP-to-reason-code mapping and the rule engine.

## 2. Intended users

Fraud strategy, underwriting operations, model risk management (validation), and internal audit.

## 3. Population and exclusions

Synthetic single-borrower mortgage purchase/refinance applications generated by Case Explorer (generator 2.0.0, baseline regime, as-of 2026-01-15). Training records: 3598 with fraud rate 0.188. The INCOHERENT data-quality negative control is labelled non-fraud. No real customer data is used.

## 6. Methodology

Four strategies were trained on identical permitted features: a deterministic rules baseline mirroring the production fraud ruleset, L2 logistic regression (standardised, class-balanced), a random forest, and gradient boosting (sklearn backend for the champion). Thresholds were tuned on the validation split to maximise F1 subject to a 5% false-positive-rate ceiling; the EV-optimal threshold from the decision-economics simulator is reported alongside. Held-out test, out-of-time, geography-stress, economic-regime-stress, adversarial and clean-negative-control splits were scored once, after tuning.

## 7. Assumptions

- Claim-vs-evidence discrepancies observed at application time are available at scoring time.
- Evidence documents are trustworthy except where the generator injects tampering.
- Loss-given-fraud and review costs in the economics module are configurable assumptions, not measured values.
- Label semantics follow the generator policy (non-material variances are review, not fraud declines).

## 14. Limitations

- Training and evaluation data are synthetic; labels are policy-derived from the generator, not observed loan outcomes.

- Bad-rate and expected-loss figures are proxies (fraud among approved x loan amount x assumed loss-given-fraud).

- Application metadata fraud signals are synthetic and separable by construction (fraud_signal_rate governs prevalence).

- Sensitive attributes are synthetic scenario labels; fairness results are technical controls, not legal findings.

- No property, appraisal, LTV or co-borrower modelling; loan amount is a fixed multiple of stated income.

- Out-of-time and stress splits are simulated regime shifts, not observed market history.
