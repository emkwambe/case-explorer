# Teaching case set: teaching-v1

_Generated by `node scripts/teaching-docs.mjs --write` from `cases.json`. Do not edit by hand._

## How the cases were generated

Every case starts from a seeded SOLAR_PRIME base case (seed `42`, as-of 2026-01-15, generator 2.0.0): the first 34 cases of the seed's blended stream that meet the base rule, in seed order. **Base rule:** Clean case approved by the credit policy (no flags) and the underwriting policy, no fraud alert, claimed FICO ≥ 680, claimed DTI ≤ 30%, claimed checking ≥ $1,000, all six evidence documents present. The cases are then produced only by the mutation engine (`src/mutations.js`); no case JSON is written by hand. `scripts/lib/teaching-set.mjs` holds the recipes and the teaching rubric, and `npm run benchmark` rebuilds the file and fails if it differs from the committed one or if its SHA-256 is missing from `benchmarks/CHANGELOG.md`.

Each case records its base (seed, index and case id), the mutations applied, the rule or threshold it tests, its **key fact**, its difficulty, and two answers:

- **expected:** the teaching answer, from the rubric for the rule the case tests. It is not the generator's truth-derived reference label, which is kept apart under `system.reference_label`.
- **system:** what the engine decides today: the Solar credit policy (`decision`, `reason_codes`), with the underwriting policy (`underwriting`) as a second column, the fraud rules that fired, and the case hash.

**Difficulty rule.**

- easy: One deciding signal at least twice its rule's threshold, or a clean case with margin.
- medium: One deciding signal within 10% above its rule's threshold, or a case one step either side of a policy threshold (FICO ±1 point, DTI ±0.1 points, checking at $999 or $1,000).
- hard: Two interacting signals, or a missing document.

**Rubric.**

- `pairClean`: Claims, truth and evidence agree and every policy threshold is met with margin: approve.
- `pairFraud`: The claims alone would pass, but one verified fact contradicts them: refer for verification (review), not approve.
- `fico`: Claimed FICO below 660 declines; 660 and above passes.
- `dti`: DTI above 45% declines; from 36% to 45% goes to review; below 36% passes.
- `liquidity`: At DTI above 40%, checking under $1,000 adds a liquidity flag; the elevated DTI already means review.
- `conflictMaterial`: Income overstated and debt understated, each above its HIGH threshold: refer for verification.
- `conflictSubThreshold`: Income overstated and debt understated, each below its HIGH threshold: together the claims cannot be relied on, so re-underwrite on verified figures (review).
- `thin`: A required document is missing: the case cannot be decided on the evidence, so review.
