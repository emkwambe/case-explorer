# Challenge mode: packs and scoring

_Generated by `node scripts/challenge-docs.mjs --write`. Do not edit by hand._

The packs are lists of `teaching-v1` case ids, derived by the rules in `src/challenge/packs.js` from the set's own fields (group, pair, difficulty, gap), never hand-picked. `challenge-data.json` holds them with the teaching entries the browser needs and the set's SHA-256 (`4f6884254c4e00b7a65a9b6b086472e76fb8743b84d05af5ca8bc88360102330`); progress saved under another SHA is kept but marked as answered against an earlier key and left out of current scores.

A session's order is seeded and deterministic (`orderPack`): a seeded shuffle, rebuilt so that no two cases from the same base case, twins or the near-threshold cases that share an applicant, are next to each other. The full set runs easy → medium → hard.

- **First week:** Easy cases only, as whole pairs: each clean approval with the fraud twin that one clear fact turns into a referral.
- **On the line:** Cases one step either side of the credit score minimum, the debt-to-income watch line and cap, and the liquidity rule.
- **Judgment calls:** Conflicting signals and missing documents.
- **Causal pairs:** Clean and fraud twins, mixed: each pair differs in one fact.
- **Where the engine is wrong:** Every case where the teaching answer and the credit policy differ, in the decision or in its reasons.
- **Full set:** All cases, easy to hard.

**Gap kinds.** Each gap case carries `gap.gap_kind`, derived from its two answers (`src/challenge/gaps.js`): "decision" when the engine decides differently (8 cases), "reasons" when it reaches the same decision with different reason codes (7 cases). The teaching set itself is not changed.

## Scoring in brief

Decision 50 (a matrix), reason codes 30 (coverage of the required codes, −10 per extra code), evidence 20 (the item that carries the deciding value); passed at 70. The full rules and worked examples are in [docs/SCORING.md](../../docs/SCORING.md).
